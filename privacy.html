# PACEPILOT Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 9, 2026 PACEPILOT is a running app that displays running pace, GPS location, a map, and step estimates. This policy explains how the app handles information. ## Information the App Uses **Location information:** PACEPILOT may access your device's location while you use GPS features to show your position on the map and support running-related features. You can control location permission through your device or browser settings. **Running information:** The app may calculate and display pace, distance, elapsed time, and step estimates during use. ## How Information Is Used Information is used to provide the app's running and mapping features. Do not assume that a run is saved or backed up unless the app explicitly provides that functionality. ## Sharing and Third-Party Services PACEPILOT does not sell personal information. The map feature may load map content from third-party map providers. Those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address and requests for map tiles, when your device loads the map. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. ## Storage and Retention PACEPILOT does not intentionally operate its own account system or server for storing your running history. Information may be processed temporarily by your device or by services needed to provide map content. ## Children's Privacy PACEPILOT is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. ## Security No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Use device and browser permissions to control access to your location. ## Your Choices You can deny or revoke location permission in your device or browser settings. Some app features may not work without location access. ## Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated as PACEPILOT changes. Updates will be published on this page. ## Contact For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the developer contact information listed on the PACEPILOT Google Play store listing.
